# Commerce evidence

Use applicable fields, not every field for every product.

## Identity and compatibility

Manufacturer/part number and GTIN/UPC where available; merchant SKU/ASIN/variant ID are source-local identifiers. Match generation, size, color, storage, region, condition, bundle and pack count. Verify accessories, dimensions, ports, OS, voltage/plug and adapters when relevant. Images/titles alone do not prove same product. Do not silently replace a model with its successor. Distinguish manufacturer refurbishment from seller refurbishment.

## Seller and authenticity

Actual sold-by business differs from marketplace operator and fulfillment provider. Verify official domain or brand distributor listing where available; unknown authorization remains unknown. Check invoice, warranty issuer, US warranty eligibility and third-party exclusions. Review seller history and implausible pricing when material; low price alone is not proof of fraud. Platform presence, HTTPS and ratings are not authenticity guarantees.

## Cost

Due-now total = item subtotal + mandatory shipping + tax + duties + mandatory fees − verified immediately applicable discounts. Compute basket shipping once at order level. Compare identical destination/currency/quantity/condition.

- Coupon eligibility, expiry, cap, exclusions, stacking and successful application.
- Member-only prices separately; membership fee/renewal never assumed.
- Cashback, points, rebates excluded from due-now total; show conditional benefit separately.
- Trade-in, financing, first-order promotions and payment-method discounts are conditional.
- Subscription intro vs renewal cost, frequency and minimum commitment; baseline is one-time purchase.
- Free-shipping thresholds do not authorize extra items.
- Import duties, conversion and foreign-transaction fees when relevant.
- Missing tax/shipping is unknown, not zero. Separate observed, estimated and conditional amounts.

## Stock and delivery

Verify exact variant and quantity, not a generic in-stock label. Distinguish preorder/backorder from ready-to-ship. Separate handling/dispatch from transit and arrival. Record destination, cutoff, timezone, business-day assumptions, delivery window and whether estimated or guaranteed. Check PO box/APO/FPO, Alaska/Hawaii/territory exclusions, signature/installation where relevant. Do not promise carrier outcomes.

## Product safety

For relevant categories such as infant products, chargers, batteries and appliances, check official recall notices against the exact model/lot/region. Exclude confirmed applicable recalled products; missing evidence is not safety certification. Use manufacturer instructions for safety-critical capability claims, not promotional copy.

## Returns and ownership

Window start, opened/used exclusions, final sale, restocking fee, return freight/address; warranty duration, responsible entity, geography and transferability. Include needed accessories, consumables, replacement parts, subscriptions and support where they change the choice. Do not promise a universal right to return an unwanted product.

## Freshness

Record source URL, observed timestamp with timezone and evidence status for material facts. Recheck finalists before purchase; no universal TTL guarantees inventory. Obey source cache/display rules. Explicit removal = removed; explicit stockout = unavailable now. CAPTCHA/403/429/timeout = unknown; back off or hand off. HTTP 200 may be a challenge page. Redirects still need identity verification.

Never refresh the successful-check timestamp after a failed attempt. Cached/search results are discovery evidence, not live purchasability. If one seller fails, try a qualifying same-product offer; if product changes, reevaluate all hard requirements.
